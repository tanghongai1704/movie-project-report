## Key Highlights

### Product Development Process in a Hackathon

- The teams shared their product development journey, from identifying problems and generating ideas to designing solutions and building prototypes.
- Learned how each team selected technologies based on the specific requirements of the problem.
- Understood the importance of planning and task allocation to ensure timely project completion within a short development cycle.

### Applying Cloud and AI in Product Development

- The projects demonstrated how Cloud and AI technologies can be combined to build scalable solutions.
- Explored how modern cloud services help reduce development time and improve deployment efficiency.
- Recognized the importance of system architecture when developing real-world applications.

### Agentic AI and the Future of Intelligent Applications

- Learned about the capabilities of Agentic AI in automating complex tasks.
- Understood how AI agents can integrate with external tools and services to create intelligent workflows.
- Recognized the growing potential of AI in modern software systems.

### Teamwork in a Hackathon Environment

- Learned how different teams organized their work and collaborated effectively.
- Understood the importance of communication, time management, and problem-solving throughout product development.
- Recognized that building a prototype quickly is an effective way to validate ideas before full-scale development.

Participating in the **"Hackathon Journey"** event was a valuable experience that provided practical insights into the process of building a technology product. Through the presentations from the participating teams, I gained a better understanding of how development teams transform ideas into real solutions by combining technology, product thinking, and effective collaboration.

### Learning from Real Projects
